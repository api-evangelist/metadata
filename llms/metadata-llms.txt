# Metadata

> Metadata is the execution layer for enterprise B2B paid campaigns. Marketing leaders use the prompt-first interface at metadata.io to ask for outcomes, and six AI agents build audiences, generate copy, creative, offers, landing pages, lead gen ads, images, and videos, draft campaigns, manage budgets, run experiments, and optimize spend across social, search, LLM, and video channels. Operators review campaign structure, assets, budget controls, and approvals in platform.metadata.io. Developers and agents can connect headlessly through Metadata MCP.
